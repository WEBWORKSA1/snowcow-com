Official rules (summary)

Eligibility

Open to individuals 18+ (or the age of majority in their jurisdiction) where contests are legal. Void where prohibited. Employees of SnowCow and prize sponsors are not eligible.

No purchase necessary

No purchase or donation is required to enter or win. A purchase or donation does not improve your chances of winning.

Entry period & limits

Monthly rounds close at 23:59 on the last day of each month; the season ends on the date shown in the countdown. Maximum one entry per person per category per month.

Judging & notification

Winners are selected by a SnowCow judging panel using the criteria above and notified by email within 14 days. Winners must respond within 7 days or an alternate is selected.

Content licence

You keep ownership of your content. By entering you grant SnowCow a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to display your entry with credit on SnowCow channels.

Prizes & taxes

Prizes are non-transferable and have no cash alternative unless stated. Winners are responsible for applicable taxes. SnowCow may substitute a prize of equal or greater value.
