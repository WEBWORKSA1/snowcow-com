Epic vs Ikon: which ski pass is right for you?

By the SnowCow Editorial Team · Updated September 2026 · 7 min read

The two biggest multi-resort passes — the Epic Pass (Vail Resorts) and the Ikon Pass (Alterra Mountain Company) — can cut the cost of a ski season by hundreds or even thousands of dollars. But buying the wrong one is an expensive mistake. Here’s the framework we use.

The 10-second answer: buy the pass that includes the resort you’ll ski the most days. Everything else is a tiebreaker.

How the two passes differ

FactorEpic PassIkon Pass
OwnerVail ResortsAlterra Mountain Company + partners
ModelMostly owned resorts, often unlimited accessOwned + partner resorts, often limited days at partners
TiersFull, Local, regional & day-based optionsFull, Base, Session & add-on options
BlackoutsOn cheaper tiers during holidaysOn Base/cheaper tiers during holidays
Best forSkiers who stick to one region or resort familyTravellers who want variety across many brands

Resort line-ups and tiers change every season. Always confirm the current resort list and blackout dates on the official pass websites before buying.

The break-even framework

  1. List your likely ski days and at which resorts.
  2. Price those days at the ticket window (or advance online price).
  3. Compare with each pass tier that covers those days, including blackout dates.
  4. Add travel value: if one pass unlocks a destination trip you’re planning, add those lift-ticket savings.

Most skiers hit break-even at roughly 4–7 days on a full pass at destination resorts, where walk-up tickets are highest. Use our trip budget calculator to run your numbers.

Choose Epic if…

  • Your home mountain is a Vail-owned resort.
  • You want unlimited days at big-name resorts without day limits.
  • You ski mostly in one region and prefer simplicity.

Choose Ikon if…

  • Your home mountain is an Ikon destination.
  • You love variety — a few days at many different resorts worldwide.
  • You travel to independent-feeling mountains (many partner resorts are iconic).

What if neither fits?

Consider the Mountain Collective, regional passes, resort-specific season passes, or advance-purchase day tickets. For a single week-long trip, a package with lift tickets included can beat both.

FAQ

Is Epic or Ikon better?

Neither is universally better. The pass covering your home mountain almost always wins.

When should I buy?

Spring and early-autumn sale windows are cheapest; prices typically rise before the season starts.

Can a pass pay off for one trip?

Often yes — if a week of window tickets costs more than the pass, buy the pass.
