The SnowCow Gear Guide

Buy once, cry once — or rent smart. Our no-hype guide to what matters, what doesn’t and what to spend at every level.

Shop by category

What to look for, and a budget / mid / premium price guide (typical retail, USD).

🎿

Skis

Match waist width to terrain: 75–90 mm for groomers, 90–105 mm all-mountain, 105 mm+ powder.

  • Budget: $300–500
  • Mid: $500–750
  • Premium: $750–1,100+
Size calculator
🏂

Snowboards

Camber = pop & edge hold. Rocker = forgiving & floaty. Hybrid = best of both for most riders.

  • Budget: $300–450
  • Mid: $450–650
  • Premium: $650–900+
Size calculator
🥾

Boots

The #1 purchase. Get fitted by a bootfitter; check flex (softer for beginners) and last width.

  • Budget: $250–400
  • Mid: $400–600
  • Premium: $600–1,000
⛑

Helmets

Look for certified models (e.g. ASTM F2040 / EN 1077) and rotational-impact systems. Replace after a significant impact.

  • Budget: $60–100
  • Mid: $100–180
  • Premium: $180–300
🥽

Goggles

Choose lens tint by light (VLT). Magnetic interchangeable lenses make flat-light days easy.

  • Budget: $40–80
  • Mid: $80–180
  • Premium: $180–300
🧥

Outerwear

10k+ mm waterproofing minimum; 20k+ for wet climates. Pit zips are underrated.

  • Budget: $100–250
  • Mid: $250–500
  • Premium: $500–900+
