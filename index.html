Frequently asked questions

Where is the most snow forecast this week?

Check Powder Watch above — it ranks 55+ major resorts by forecast snowfall for the next 7 days, pulled live from weather-model data every time you load the page.

Is the SnowCow trip quote really free?

Yes. You tell us your dates, group and budget; we match you with vetted ski travel specialists who send options. There is no fee and no obligation.

How accurate are the snow forecasts?

They come from global weather models adjusted to mid-mountain elevation. They're reliable for spotting storms 1–5 days out. Always check the resort's official snow report and local avalanche bulletin before heading out.

Can my resort, shop or brand advertise on SnowCow?

Absolutely — we offer display, sponsored guides, newsletter placements, contest sponsorship and qualified trip leads. See the media kit.
