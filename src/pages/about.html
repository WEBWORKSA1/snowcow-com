About SnowCow

Why a cow? Because cows are calm, reliable, and always find the best pasture. We do the same for snow.

Our mission

Planning a ski trip is confusing: dozens of passes, hundreds of resorts, forecasts that disagree and prices that change daily. SnowCow cuts through the noise with live data, honest guides and free human help.

What we believe

  • Data first — forecasts and tools before opinions.
  • Independent — we label every sponsored placement and never sell reviews.
  • Free for skiers — funded by advertising, partners and community supporters.
  • Safety matters — we always point you to official reports and avalanche bulletins.

How we make money

Transparently: display advertising (including Google AdSense), affiliate links, sponsorships, partner referral fees on trip quotes, and supporter contributions. None of these affect our forecasts or rankings.
