Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

  • Information you submit in forms (trip quotes, newsletter, contests, applications, pledges, inquiries): name, email, phone, country, trip details and messages.
  • Usage data via cookies and similar technologies (pages viewed, device, approximate location) when you consent to analytics.
  • Preferences stored only in your browser (theme, units, saved resorts).

How we use it

  • To respond to you and deliver requested services (e.g. sharing a trip request with relevant travel specialists to prepare quotes — only with your consent).
  • To send newsletters and alerts you opted into (unsubscribe anytime).
  • To run contests, recruit contributors and process pledges.
  • To measure and improve the site, and to show advertising.

Advertising & Google AdSense

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses data from partner sites.

Service providers

Forms are delivered via a third-party form-processing service; hosting is provided by GitHub Pages; forecasts by Open-Meteo; videos by YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode, loaded only when you press play); fonts by Google Fonts; optional analytics by Google Analytics. Each processes data under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA/Québec Law 25), you may request access, correction, deletion or portability of your data, and object to or withdraw consent for processing. Use our contact page. We do not sell personal information.

Retention & security

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purpose collected (typically up to 24 months for leads and inquiries) and use reasonable safeguards. No internet transmission is 100% secure.

Children

SnowCow is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect their data. Contest entrants must be adults.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “last updated” date will change accordingly.
