Level 4

  • Trees
  • Avalanche education

Lessons vs. teaching yourself

Most people learn roughly twice as fast with a qualified instructor for their first 1–3 days. Group lessons are the best value; private lessons fix habits fastest. Self-teaching is possible — but bad habits (leaning back, “A-frame” turns) are hard to unlearn.

  • First-timer: 2–3 half-day group lessons
  • Stuck at intermediate: 1 private lesson
  • Going off-piste: take an avalanche course (e.g. AST 1 / AIARE 1)

Mountain safety essentials

🧭 Know the code

Stay in control. People ahead have the right of way. Stop where you’re visible. Look uphill before merging. Use devices to prevent runaway equipment. Obey signs & closures. Know how to use lifts safely.

🏔 Respect the backcountry

Beyond the ropes there’s no avalanche control. Carry beacon, shovel & probe, go with trained partners and check the local avalanche forecast.

🥶 Cold, sun & altitude

Layer up, protect skin with SPF 30+, drink water, and ease into high altitude. Head down if you feel severe headache, nausea or confusion.
