Your first ski trip: the complete beginner’s guide

By the SnowCow Editorial Team · Updated September 2026 · 9 min read

Your first ski trip should be fun, not overwhelming. Follow this guide and you’ll arrive knowing exactly what to book, what to wear and what to expect on day one.

1. Choose a beginner-friendly resort

Look for a dedicated learning area with a magic carpet, lots of wide green runs, a well-reviewed ski school and lodging close to the lifts. Giant resorts famous for steep terrain can still work — but only if they have a strong beginner zone. Browse our beginner-friendly resorts.

2. What to book (and in what order)

  1. Lodging — close to the lifts or ski school saves energy and time.
  2. Lessons — book 2–3 half-day group lessons in advance; they sell out on holidays.
  3. Rentals — reserve online for lower prices and faster pickup.
  4. Lift tickets — beginners often only need a cheaper learning-area ticket on day one.
  5. Travel insurance — make sure it covers winter sports.

3. What to wear

  • Moisture-wicking base layer (no cotton!)
  • Warm mid layer (fleece or light puffy)
  • Waterproof jacket & pants
  • One pair of thin-to-medium ski socks
  • Waterproof gloves or mittens, neck gaiter, helmet & goggles

Full details in our layering guide.

4. Day one: what actually happens

You’ll collect rentals, meet your instructor, and learn to walk, glide, stop and turn on gentle terrain. Most adults can link basic turns on green runs by the end of day two or three. Snowboarders often find day one harder and day three much easier — stick with it.

5. Rookie mistakes to avoid

  • Skipping lessons to “save money” — it usually costs more days of frustration.
  • Wearing two pairs of socks (cold toes, sore shins).
  • Skiing until exhausted — most injuries happen on the last run.
  • Forgetting sunscreen — UV is intense at altitude.
  • Following friends onto runs above your level.
