Trip quote FAQ

Is the ski trip quote free?

Yes — 100% free with no obligation to book. Our partners pay us, never you.

Who will contact me?

A SnowCow trip coordinator reviews your request and shares it only with vetted specialists relevant to your destination. You won’t be spammed.

How fast will I get options?

Most travellers receive first options within 24–48 hours; peak holiday dates can take a little longer.

Can you help me choose where to go?

Definitely. Type “Help me choose” as the destination and tell us your priorities — we’ll suggest resorts that fit your budget and ability.
